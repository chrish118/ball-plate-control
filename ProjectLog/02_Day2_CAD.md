# Day 2 — Initial arm and top-plate CAD

I started modelling the mechanical parts in Fusion 360, focusing on the **top plate** and the **first version of the servo arms**.

For the top plate, I worked on the square plate geometry, the recessed centre and the four corner mounting features. I also modelled four separate arm components, each with a hole at the upper end for the intended linkage arrangement.

At this stage, the aim was to get the basic geometry into CAD and see how the parts would fit together. The arm design was still an early iteration. During the following session, I realised the arms were too short for the intended mechanism and redesigned them.
